The hook that works

Do not tell them they are invisible. Show them who AI recommends instead.

The pattern from our live campaigns

"We asked ChatGPT for the best dentist in Austin. It named three practices. You were not one of them, and one of them has a third of your reviews."

Call to action: reply "show me". Then send the real answer as proof before asking for any time.

  • It is specific and checkable. They can run the same question themselves in ten seconds.
  • It names competitors. Losing to a rival creates urgency that "AI is the future" never will.
  • It is a proof artifact that travels. The owner forwards it to a partner without needing us in the room.
  • It answers the hardest SPICED gap. AI visibility has no natural deadline. A competitor being named today is the deadline.
  • Send more info means send the screenshot, not a deck. Documents get buried.

Objections

The six you will hear in week one.

"Is this cloaking?"

Cloaking means showing Google something different to game Google. Googlebot gets the normal site. AI crawlers get a structured version of the same approved facts. Answer it directly, never dodge it.

"We already do SEO"

Keep doing it. SEO is for Google. AI engines read and pick businesses differently, which is why most AI cited pages sit outside Google's top 100. The two work together.

"We use Profound / a tracker"

Good, use it as our scorecard. Trackers report the gap. Appear changes what AI receives and hosts it. We include competitor tracking anyway.

"Why $2,500 when tools cost $99?"

Tools report, we fix and host. Rebuild the maths with their own cost per lead and customer value from discovery. Then negotiate on value, not discount.

"Can you guarantee results?"

Nobody controls what AI says. We control what AI crawlers receive and we measure it. Plans target measurable results in 30 to 90 days depending on tier.

"You're small"

True, we are young. The risk of trying is low: one DNS record, nothing changes on the site, remove it to undo. Never claim a certification we do not hold.
