How it works

Two audiences, one website. We call it adaptive rendering.

Websites are built for people: images, scripts, menus. AI crawlers read raw text, so most of what makes a business worth recommending is invisible to them. AppearAI runs a delivery layer alongside the site, on its own hostname. People see the site exactly as before. AI crawlers get a version built for machines: clear structure, direct answers to real buyer questions, services and locations stated plainly.

Setup is one DNS record. Typical example:
TypeCNAMENameappearValueproxy.joinappear.comResultappear.yourdomain.com

The account manager confirms the exact record per site. On Cloudflare the proxy must be set to DNS only.

  • Coverage: track citations across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude

What AppearAI is not

Not SEO. SEO ranks a page in a list. We get a business named inside a generated answer. Not a monitoring tool. Trackers report. We change what AI can read. Not instant. Answer engines recrawl on their own schedule.

Objections

The ones you will hear in week one.

Is this cloaking?

Cloaking means showing Google something different to game Google. Googlebot gets the normal site. AI crawlers get a structured version of the same approved facts. Answer it head on.

We already do SEO

Keep doing it. SEO is for Google. AI engines read and choose businesses differently. The two work together. Never bash their agency.

Tools cost $99. Why $2,500?

Tools report, we fix and host. Rebuild the maths with their own cost per lead and customer value from discovery.

Can you guarantee results?

No one controls what AI says. We control what AI crawlers receive and we measure it. Plans carry result targets, not promises.

You are small

True, we are young. Trying is low risk: one DNS record, nothing changes on the site, remove it to undo. Never claim a certification we do not hold.

Send me some info

Do not send a deck. Send one specific, checkable observation: what ChatGPT says when asked for the best in their city. Or offer two call times.

If a customer cancels: remove the record and routing returns to normal. What AI already learned may stay, but no new improvements reach new crawls.

The hook that works

Do not tell them they are invisible. Show them who AI recommends instead.

First line, from the sourcing playbook

"We checked who ChatGPT recommends for HVAC in Dallas. 3 competitors come up more often than you."

The first line is news, not a pitch. It is specific, checkable in ten seconds, and it names rivals. That is the critical event AI visibility otherwise lacks.

Rules for every claim

  • Rank based claims only: "comes up less often than". Never "you are invisible".
  • Save every ChatGPT answer with its date. Answers change between runs.
  • Never mention the business whose audit surfaced the lead.
  • Talk about patients, clients and bookings, not crawlers and schema.
  • Branch on replies and acceptances, never on opens. Keep open tracking off.
