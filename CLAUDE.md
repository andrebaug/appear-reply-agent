# Appear AI: standing instructions

## Brand guidelines (ALWAYS apply to anything visual or customer facing)

Source of truth: Notion page "Appear brand guidelines" (Appear AI)
https://app.notion.com/p/413348e977d5813abd53d78cb819f5f7
(it sits in the private "Memories" database, so normal Notion search can miss it; fetch it by this URL).
It holds the official 38 slide "Appear AI Brand Guidelines" PDF and the logo files (PNG and SVG).
Never take branding from joinappear.com code, old Drive files, or skill defaults (for example blue #4361ee and Poppins are outdated).

- Name: ALWAYS write "Appear AI" in all text (never just "Appear"). The logo artwork is the official "AppearAI" wordmark with the sunrise icon; use the files as they are. Use the official logo files only. Black logo on light backgrounds, white logo on dark backgrounds or photos. Never recolour, stretch, crop or change opacity.
- Creative direction: "Nature Bathing". Calm, clear, confident. Covers and section openers use soft focus, grainy, blurred nature photos (deep greens with warm yellow and orange glow); logo in white on top.
- Content slides: white background with a subtle visible grid (website construction feel), flat Soft Linen cards with square corners, small grey pill labels, lots of space.
- Primary colours: Midnight #090909, Daylight #FFFFFF, Forest Shade #0A4D2C, New Leaf #DBFF65 (highlight card), Soft Linen #F7F6EE.
- Secondary (accents only): Evergreen #007C55, Sunlight #FCD901, Sunset Orange #F26621, Open Sky #155ABE, Wild Rose #F6B5B6, Morning Mist #B1DFF3.
- Gradients: 1 Sunlight > Evergreen > Forest Shade > Midnight. 2 Soft Linen > Morning Mist > Open Sky > Midnight. 3 Soft Linen > Sunlight > Sunset Orange. 4 Wild Rose > Sunset Orange. 5 Wild Rose > Open Sky.
- Type: Darker Grotesque for everything (headings, body, UI). Geist Mono only for small technical labels, sources, code.
- Imagery: modernised impressionism, blurred colourful nature. Illustration: airbrush and coloured pencil, handmade feel.

## Deliverables

- PDFs: the user's viewer cannot open PDFs with embedded web fonts. Build PDFs from slide images, and also publish an Artifact link (slides viewable in the browser plus PDF download) so the file always opens.
- Writing style: no hyphens or em dashes, plain human language.
